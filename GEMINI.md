# Antigravity Rules

## Communication Language
- **ALWAYS respond in English.**
- Even if the user communicates in Telugu, transliterated Telugu, or any other language, ALWAYS formulate and deliver all thoughts, responses, explanations, drafts, and answers strictly in English. Never reply in Telugu.

## Punctuation and Formatting Rules
- **NEVER use the em-dash "—" (U+2014) in any drafts, responses, emails, connection notes, or text.**
- Always use commas, parentheses, colons, or standard hyphens ("-") instead.
